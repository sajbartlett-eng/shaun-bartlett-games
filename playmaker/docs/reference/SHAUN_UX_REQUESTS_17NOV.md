# Shaun's UX requests (17 Nov 2026), in his words

> I want a full UX, legibility and accessibility audit and improvement prompt.

**1. Moving players and taking actions**

> My personal requests. A much more streamlined experience of moving players around and completing actions. You can suggest otherwise, but I'm thinking single tap/click for movement options and single click to place and then confirm, double click for turn ending actions on the player completing the action, then a selection menu that doesn't obscure the board, single click placement/direction and confirmation. For changing arrow direction a simple click and drag rotate function would be ideal or click to rotate 45 degrees and confirm, though that may be too slow.

**2. Coaching and scouting in advantages**

> I also want clear plain language coaching and scouting with the current ratings view available on click. People don't need to think in terms of this number vs this number. They need to think in terms of advantages. This is what he's good at. This is what the other is bad at. Exploit the difference. And the people will probably generally understand percentage chance of success with very clear, concise and subtle "why" rather than being bombarded with numbers. I always intended the numbers to be for resolution but the colours (above average, average and below average) coupled with the player types to drive 90% of decision making. That builds intuitive scouting for real basketball.

**3. Turn-by-turn play diagrams, the coach's assessments and a report**

> Another request is to change the output play mark-ups to be turn by turn rather than phase by phase and numbered "Turn 1", "Turn 2" accompanied by a one line description of what each team did strategically. And at the end of each phase, a coach's assessment of the phase for both teams, leading into the next phase. And at the end a coach assessment of the whole possession with shot quality breakdown. This should be downloadable as a report.

**4. A simple home screen**

> I would also like a real simplification of options on the home screen. Default settings, hide advanced options behind advanced settings.

**5. Learning through the first game**

> Improve the onboarding and allow players to learn through the first game instead of a separate training.

**6. Watching the computer play itself**

> I would also like to restore the option for computer to play computer so that I can see how they set up, see how they play, and audit the reports I download for insights to improve the computer systems.

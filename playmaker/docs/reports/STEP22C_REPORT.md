## Choices your words didn't settle

1. **The closely guarded count:** a third, two thirds, all of the -5 as the count goes 1, 2, 3. It only bites once he has picked up his dribble, because moving restarts the count.
2. **The shot clock:** you asked for the game's own phase values. That table rises from 24.9 at the first phase to 29.5 at the last, so a late possession would read better for the offence. I counted the clock by the rules instead.
3. **The race:** a tie in phases goes to the defender. Bodies and walls on the way are not counted beyond this phase. The pass's chance is read as the defence stands now. The late defender's contest is counted from in front.
4. **How long a race runs:** on real boards almost every race is decided in one phase. Only with the offence bunched at mid-court and the defence in a corner did any run to two.
5. **Matchup words.** "X1: on O2" names whoever covers that attacker's most valuable look, which can be a defender far from him (X1 in the paint for the Sharpshooter's drive, while X3 stands beside him). A fix is ready if you want it: name the nearest defender who takes something off his look.
6. **Sense checks 2 and 3.** I suggest measuring them by position (he stands in the key; the pass to the Sharpshooter is cut) in place of the job-line words.
7. **A contest one Move away counts as set,** the same as standing beside the shooter (D-222). That is why the search reads little difference between a hand in his face and a defender three squares off. If ball pressure should be worth more, this is the rule to look at.
8. **Setup time:** 6 to 13 seconds. The last pass is the cost. D-200 waives speed, so I left it in.

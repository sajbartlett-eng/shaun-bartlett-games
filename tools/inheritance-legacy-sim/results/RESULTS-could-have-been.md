## What could have been

Family wealth is both siblings' final wealth together. "Could have held" adds back the four losses the game can account for: Property not inherited because of the fence, payments from holdings the fence divided, estates that left the family, and the best available purchase a sibling with Capital did not make. The fairest fence is the solver's smallest possible gap on that estate; "not available" estates are too large for the plot-by-plot search.

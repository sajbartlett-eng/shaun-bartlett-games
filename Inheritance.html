    Inheritance

    A competitive I-cut-you-choose game for two.

    1. Build

    Build an estate together by placing assets.

    Rock, Paper, Scissors decides who will cut and who will choose.
    2. Cut

    The Cutter carefully draws a line to split the estate's value.

    3. Choose

    The Chooser picks a side.

    The owner of the higher-value property wins. A perfect split goes to the Cutter.
